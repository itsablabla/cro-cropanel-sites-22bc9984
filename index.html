Voice of customer

What customers actually say

Gathered from third-party reviews, forums and video — the site’s own pages are excluded. Every quote was verified word-for-word against the page it links to; anything that could not be verified was discarded rather than paraphrased.

Said before buying — these are conversion problems

Hidden fees and surprise charges
Instead, I was hit with a surprise charge of $139 just a few months later, $40 more than expected, without any prior notice given that my rate was going up.
— gratefulglamper.com
Prospects are not told about equipment rental fees or rate increases, leading to unexpected bills and distrust.
Misleading equipment ownership terms
I bought the router outright, but 8 months later, I suddenly got charged $25/month rental fees.
— ie.trustpilot.com
Customers believe they are buying the router outright, but later learn it is rented, causing confusion and frustration.
Misrepresentation of service coverage
They said they would ship two Sims with the modem and I could pick whichever had the better coverage. Paid for the modem and plan, and received a Verizon modem and Sim.
— ie.trustpilot.com
Prospects are told they can choose carriers but receive a different one, leading to unusable service and wasted money.

Said after buying — these shape what the page must reassure

Unclear cancellation process
The official cancellation process could not be completed without the modem model and serial number, which meant I still had an unresolved situation.
— dbakevlar.com
Customers cannot find how to cancel or are told they must return equipment they never received, making it hard to leave.
Unresponsive and unhelpful customer support
The worst service..impossible to talk to human support..all chat and text doesn't get anything solved.
— bbb.org
Prospects expect reliable support but encounter chatbots, no phone support, and repeated explanations, increasing risk.
Billing errors and double charges
they double-charged me with no refund despite multiple attempts.
— bbb.org
Customers are charged for services they canceled or never authorized, leading to financial disputes and distrust.
